01 About the Meet

Years After Leaving JNU, the Connection Endures

The SC&SS Alumni Meet 2026 is an opportunity to walk down familiar campus pathways, catch up with batchmates, meet current scholars, and strengthen the alumni network.

Whether you graduated in the early batches of MCA and M.Tech or recently completed your Ph.D., your presence enriches the school's legacy.

Event Highlights

  • Inaugural & Welcome Address by School Leadership
  • Felicitation of Former Deans & Revered Faculty
  • Open Dialogues on Systems, AI & Industry Evolutions
  • Batch Group Photographs & Memory Walks
  • High Tea & Networking Lunch at JNU Campus
  • Alumni Mentorship & Collaboration Initiatives
02 The SC&SS Story

A Tradition of Scientific Inquiry & Systems Leadership

Established as one of the premier computer and systems science schools in India, SC&SS JNU has continuously fostered rigorous academic inquiry and technological innovation.

Pillar 01

Academic Rigor

Rooted in mathematical foundations, theoretical computer science, databases, and distributed systems, producing thinkers who excel worldwide.

Pillar 02

Vibrant Alumni

Graduates leading technology organizations, research laboratories, universities, government bodies, and innovative startups globally.

Pillar 03

Future Vision

Advancing research in artificial intelligence, cyber-physical systems, quantum computing, and data sciences while nurturing the next generation.
